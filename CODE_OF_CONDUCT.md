# Code of Conduct

Stealth is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, inclusive, and considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Discuss ideas and code directly without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Protect private information and do not share credentials, customer data, or undisclosed vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized or hateful conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private or security-sensitive information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Do not use a public issue for sensitive details. Maintainers may edit, remove, or close contributions and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
